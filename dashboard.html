Act this month
The four long-lead items
  • Upload the resume. Blocks real fit scoring and all per-role tailoring. Nothing downstream is fully useful until this exists.
  • Email UT Austin Texas Global about signing a convenio de prácticas. This is a tripartite agreement between you, a Spanish employer, and the university — without the university's signature there is no legal path to Madrid or Barcelona. One email either unblocks four target companies or kills the market. Longest lead time of anything here.
  • Start the Australian Work and Holiday visa (subclass 462) if Australia is in play. US citizens qualify at 18–30, partway through tertiary education counts, no government support letter needed, English test waived, ~AUD $640. It is not employer-sponsored, so you can and should apply before having an offer. The US allocation is capped near 2,500 places a year and closes when hit.
  • Confirm Masa eligibility for Israel — their programs require proof of Judaism. If you don't qualify, Israel closes and that effort reallocates.
  • Apply to all live Tier A roles. Eight are open right now.
  • Big 4 advisory spring applications open Aug–Sept. Deloitte, PwC, EY, KPMG all run analytics-flavored spring cycles. Rolling review — seats fill before the posted deadline.
  • Draft your cold-outreach templates now so September is execution, not writing.

Corporate posting season
  • The densest posting window of the year across large employers — Amazon, Apple, the Big 4, and most structured corporate programs open here.
  • Most of this volume is Summer 2027, but spring and off-cycle roles ride the same recruiting calendar, so it's where the corporate side of your pipeline actually fills.
